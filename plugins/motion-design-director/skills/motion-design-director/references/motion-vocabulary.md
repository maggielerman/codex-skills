# Motion Vocabulary

Use these profiles to turn subjective feedback into implementable direction.

## Soft

- Feel: calm, gentle, quiet.
- Timing: base to slow duration.
- Easing: smooth ease-out, no hard snap.
- Distance: micro to subtle.
- Stagger: tight or base.
- Avoid: bounce, large travel, abrupt masks.

## Tactile

- Feel: responsive, physical, direct.
- Timing: micro to fast.
- Easing: decisive ease-out or very slight overshoot.
- Distance: micro.
- Stagger: usually none.
- Use for: buttons, toggles, cards, drag, hover, press feedback.
- Avoid: delayed response after input.

## Editorial

- Feel: authored, paced, hierarchical.
- Timing: base to slow.
- Easing: entrance or in-out.
- Distance: subtle for text, base for media.
- Stagger: base or loose, with primary objects leading.
- Use for: section entrances, story scroll, feature reveals, portfolio surfaces.
- Avoid: equal timing across all elements.

## Magical

- Feel: surprising but coherent.
- Timing: base to cinematic.
- Easing: smooth in-out, path-following, mask, morph, or continuity-based.
- Distance: based on object relationship, not arbitrary travel.
- Use for: object transformation, guided paths, masks, morphs, progressive disclosure.
- Avoid: random motion without visible cause and effect.

## Premium

- Feel: confident, restrained, polished.
- Timing: fast to base for UI, base to slow for editorial.
- Easing: entrance, standard, or custom but not default-looking.
- Distance: micro to subtle for text, base for media.
- Stagger: tight or base.
- Avoid: elastic bounce, overlong delays, noisy parallax, every-card choreography.

## Playful

- Feel: expressive, responsive, charming.
- Timing: fast to base.
- Easing: subtle overshoot when appropriate.
- Distance: subtle to expressive.
- Stagger: base or loose.
- Use for: exploratory tools, creative surfaces, games, low-risk delight.
- Avoid: conflicting with serious or dense operational tasks.

## Cinematic

- Feel: staged, immersive, spatial.
- Timing: slow to cinematic.
- Easing: in-out or scroll-scrubbed.
- Distance: base to expressive.
- Stagger: loose.
- Use for: hero scenes, scroll narratives, parallax depth, pinned scenes.
- Avoid: capturing scroll for too long or hiding the next action.

## Utility

- Feel: clear, quick, functional.
- Timing: instant to fast.
- Easing: standard.
- Distance: micro or none.
- Stagger: none or tight.
- Use for: dashboards, forms, tables, repeated workflows.
- Avoid: decorative motion that slows task completion.
