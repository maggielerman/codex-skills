# Product brief: Codex Skills Packs

## 1) Problem

People are building serious workflows with Codex, but reusable operating patterns often stay trapped in one local setup. Buyers need portable, supported skills and plugin packs that can be installed into their own repos without reverse-engineering Maggie's local environment.

## 2) Target customer

- Operators, founders, developers, and agent-heavy teams who want reusable Codex workflows.
- Customers who need installable repo workflows, not just prompt snippets.

## 3) MVP offer

- Curated Codex skills pack.
- Custom plugin backups where relevant.
- Customer-facing docs site with setup, per-skill guides, plugin guides, and troubleshooting.
- Clear commercial licensing posture before public sale.

## 4) Success criteria

- A customer can identify the right skill or plugin for their workflow.
- A customer can install the pack without losing bundled scripts, assets, or references.
- A customer can troubleshoot common trigger, path, and repo-context issues.
- The shipped catalog matches `skills/manifest.json` and plugin metadata.
