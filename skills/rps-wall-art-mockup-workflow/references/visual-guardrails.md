# Visual Guardrails

## RPS Direction

- Editorial, modern, art-forward, and believable.
- Let the artwork be the reason to click.
- Avoid generic stock interiors, anonymous filler art, overdecorated rooms, harsh bloom, and fake gallery clutter.
- Use realistic frame sizes, spacing, perspective, and scale.
- Prefer compositions that show how the artwork lives in a room without burying the art.

## Named Artists And Artwork

- Generate blank-placeholder scenes only.
- Composite real RPS source/product art into the placeholders.
- Preserve recognizable artwork composition.
- Do not imitate an artist's style through generation.

## Shopify Listing Images

- Make the product understandable quickly.
- Show clear art, believable scale, and useful room context.
- Do not imply framed fulfillment if only prints are sold.
- Use alt-ready file naming and manifest metadata.

## Editorial And Collection Images

- Stronger mood is acceptable, but avoid vague decorative art.
- If a collection, palette, or subject is named, the visible artwork should support that exact claim.
